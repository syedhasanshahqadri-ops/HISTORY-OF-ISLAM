Chronology

Across the Centuries

  1. 632

    The Rashidun era begins with Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA).

  2. 661

    Muawiya I (RA) begins the Umayyad caliphate.

  3. 750

    The Umayyad era ends with Marwan II.

  4. c. 1299

    Osman I begins the Ottoman line of sultans.

  5. 1922

    The sultanate ends with Mehmed VI.

About the Project

Built to Read, Search and Share

Two Languages

Every archive reads in English and Urdu, with the Jameel Noori Nastaleeq script for Urdu.

Search Every Ruler

Find a ruler by name, then search inside their profile for wars, death, sons, daughters and more.

Light and Dark

Read in a bright parchment theme or a deep midnight theme. Your choice carries across all the archives.

Offline Access

Install the App

Install History of Islam on your phone, tablet or computer. Once installed, all three archives open without an internet connection.
